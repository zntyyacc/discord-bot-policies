# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 2, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Dino XBT (the "Bot") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Automatically Collected Information
- Discord User ID and username
- Message content sent to the Bot
- Timestamp of interactions
- Command usage statistics

### 2.2 User-Provided Information
- Wallet addresses (when provided for transactions)
- Transaction parameters and preferences
- Custom configuration settings

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- Private keys or seed phrases
- Personal identification documents
- Payment card information
- Email addresses (unless voluntarily provided)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide and improve Bot functionality
- Execute requested blockchain transactions
- Maintain conversation context
- Analyze usage patterns for service optimization
- Troubleshoot technical issues

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored on secure servers with encryption at rest
- Conversation history is retained for service continuity
- Sensitive data (API keys, tokens) is encrypted using industry-standard methods
- We implement access controls to limit data exposure

## 5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell or share your personal information with third parties, except:
- When required by law or legal process
- To blockchain networks (transaction data is public by design)
- With service providers necessary for Bot operation (e.g., hosting services)

## 6. Third-Party Services

The Bot interacts with third-party blockchain networks and APIs. These services have their own privacy policies:
- Blockchain networks (Ethereum, Solana, etc.) — transaction data is public
- DeFi protocols — smart contract interactions are on-chain
- Market data providers — may log API requests

## 7. Data Retention

- Conversation logs: Retained for 90 days
- Transaction history: Retained indefinitely for audit purposes
- User preferences: Retained until account deletion

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Request access to your stored data
- Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
- Opt-out of data collection by discontinuing use of the Bot
- Update or correct your information

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the Bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## 11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data requests, please contact us via GitHub issues at:
https://github.com/zntyyacc/discord-bot-policies/issues
